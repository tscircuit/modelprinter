# Insulating Snap-On Busbar Cover (`busbarcover`)

Open-ended extruded U-channel protective busbar shroud with inward lower snap lips.

```text
busbarcover_l100mm_iw30mm_ih20mm_t2mm_lip2mm_inwarduchannel
```

## Local Coordinate Datum

- **Origin `(0, 0, 0)`**: Lower lip seating plane center (`bottomZ = 0`, `topZ = innerHeight + thickness`).
