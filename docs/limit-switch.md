# Industrial Roller-Lever Limit Switch (`limitswitch`)

Rectangular industrial limit switch enclosure with rotary roller lever arm, 2-hole body mounting, and bottom conduit thread.

- **Origin `(0, 0, 0)`**: Rear mounting face center of switch housing (`backY = 0`, `bottomZ = 0`).
