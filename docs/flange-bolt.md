# flangebolt parameter contract

```ts
mp.string("flangebolt_headstandard(iso4162)_m6_l25mm_fullthread_plainface").json()
```

This family pins the **head and flange envelope** to ISO 4162:2012, supporting M5, M6, M8 and M10. `headstandard(iso4162:2012)` selects the same edition. It deliberately uses `headstandard`, because the requested fully threaded M6 x 25 variant extends ISO 4162's standard 18 mm thread length. `standard(iso4162)` is rejected with a message directing callers to the head selector. This is nominal assembly geometry, not a claim that a fully threaded fastener complies with every ISO 4162 requirement.

Sources are [ISO 4162:2012 Figures 1–3](https://cdn.standards.iteh.ai/samples/56451/10d71b8d8c634e9790d4ddcf4c296cc2/ISO-4162-2012.pdf) and [Nufast's dated 2014 production drawing extracting the 2012 dimension table](https://www.nufast.co.uk/uploads/ISO4162.pdf). `flangeboltDimensions` uses nominal thread diameter/pitch and across-flats, maximum head height/flange diameter, minimum flange thickness and minimum under-head radius. M6 has a 13.6 mm flange and a 6.8 mm total head height; these are the small-series ISO envelope, not the larger DIN 6921 envelope.

The manufacturer-dependent outline is fixed explicitly: a cylindrical flange rim of the listed thickness, a 22.5-degree upper conical shoulder, a regular hex head, and a 30-degree top chamfer ending at a circular face whose diameter equals across-flats. The shoulder joins the hex where their radial surfaces intersect. Optional head indentation, flange edge rounding, hex-to-flange blending, tolerances, coatings and markings are omitted. The under-head circular fillet has the listed radius. The terminal 45-degree chamfer has equal axial/radial setback P/2. The root is flattened rather than rounded: with H=sqrt(3)*P/2, pitch diameter is d-3H/4 and root diameter is d-17H/12. These visual choices are exported by modelprinter and consumed by the renderer.

The flat plain bearing face is Z=0. The shank extends to Z=-length; the entire flange and head occupy 0 through headHeight. Length excludes the head and must exceed underHeadRadius+tipChamfer. Hex flats lie at Y=+/-headAcrossFlats/2. The single-start crest crosses +X at Z=-length before tip clipping; right-handed threads advance counterclockwise as Z increases. `lefthanded` reverses this. `nothreads` retains the major-diameter envelope and all other features.

`fullthread` and `plainface` normalize to true `fullThread` and `plainFace` booleans, which default true. Partial threading and serrated faces are unsupported. `m`/`metricsize(mN)`, `l`/`length`, `d`/`diameter`, and `af`/`headacrossflats` are aliases. `threads`/`nothreads` and `lefthanded`/`righthanded` are mutually exclusive value-free flags. Unknown tokens, duplicate properties (including aliases), flags with values and conflicting dimensional overrides are rejected. Schemas reject unknown properties and false shape flags. Decimal lengths accept mm, cm, m, in or inch; output uses millimeters. Angles use unitless degrees. Complete values are required; exponent notation, whitespace in schema lengths and trailing junk are rejected. Exported props/definition schemas accept their own normalized outputs.
